## 生成プロンプト

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide decorative hero illustration for a Japanese day-trip planner named Yorimichi. Primary request: inviting premium editorial travel illustration, a small Japanese train crossing a curving stone bridge over a turquoise river, a quiet traditional shopping lane with a tiny cafe and people exploring, green rolling hills, a vermilion torii peeking through trees, a small temple roof. This is a conceptual collage of a relaxing day out, not a depiction of an actual destination. Style: hand-painted gouache on soft textured cream paper, refined travel magazine illustration, simplified elegant forms, subtle printed texture, natural sunny afternoon. Palette: warm ivory #f6f5f1, deep forest green #244b3d, sage, muted terracotta #bf765e, soft ochre, turquoise. Composition: landscape 3:2, scene fills canvas but airy, no huge text or title area needed (copy will sit separately beside it). No lettering, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no photo borders. Rich crafted detail with restrained color.
